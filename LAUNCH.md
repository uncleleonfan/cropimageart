## Description（产品描述）

```
A fast, beautiful, and private image cropping tool that works entirely in your browser.

✨ Features:
• Drag & drop or paste images — instantly ready
• 9 built-in aspect ratios (1:1, 4:3, 16:9, 3:2, 9:16, and more)
• Freeform crop + lock ratio toggle
• Rotate 90° CW/CCW, flip horizontal/vertical
• Gesture support: pinch-zoom, scroll-wheel, double-click
• Real-time preview at the bottom
• Download in original quality — PNG or JPG
• Dark mode by default (because it's 2026)

🔒 Privacy-first: Your images never leave your device. Everything runs client-side on Canvas — no server upload, no tracking, no account needed.

🌐 Works offline after first load. PWA-ready.

Built with Next.js, open-source on GitHub.
```
